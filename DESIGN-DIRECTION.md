# Direction contract — Closingly app surface

Development-only contract. DESIGN.md is written at finish, from the built world.

Mode: **Operate**. Supersedes the earlier Drafting Table direction, which the
user pinned out: it was blue and hard-edged, and both were wrong. A user pin
beats the roll.

## THESIS

Closingly turns a loose conversation into committed revenue. The interface
should feel worked and warm — something handled daily by one person who owns
the outcome — not a cold analytics console. It refuses the category default on
two axes at once: the blue-accented SaaS palette every competitor ships, and
the machined, hard-edged grid that signals "dashboard" rather than "workshop".

## OWN-WORLD

Ground is a near-white paper with the faintest warmth in it, never a flat
white. Ink is a deep graphite, never pure black. The accent is a steel blue,
carried in two steps because one mid-tone cannot do both jobs: `brand`
(#2b6ba8, holds text and fills controls, measured 5.34:1 on the ground) and
`brand-vivid` (#3985d4, for marks, icons, charts and borders, measured 3.67:1
against the 3:1 non-text bar). A muted slate carries the second data series
without competing. Edges are soft — 10px base radius, nothing sharp — because
the world is handled, not machined. Surfaces are panels with a real
offset-and-blur shadow, not hairline plates.

Type is a pairing, not a single face: **Fraunces** for display, an old-style
serif with SOFT and WONK axes engaged, which is the clearest single signal that
this was not assembled from a component gallery; **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body
and UI, humanist with soft terminals where Inter is neutral; **DM Mono** for
tabular figures and metadata only. All three self-hosted through next/font.

## STORY

The consultant opens the workspace and it feels like their own bench —
familiar and specific to them. They see what is committed and what is drifting,
understand why the forecast reads what it reads, and act on the one thing marked
in blue. Nothing else competes for that attention.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Left rail: workspace, the six destinations, assistant at the base. Main surface
opens with the day's obligation as a sentence set in Fraunces at display scale —
no eyebrow above it, that is banned. Beneath, the figures that govern the week
set tabular and tight, compared rather than spaced out to look calm. Then the
attention list, the only place the vivid blue appears, each row carrying
its reason and one action at the right margin.

## FORM

Steel. The palette was pinned by the user to the blue accent in their own
screenshot; the persimmon that preceded it and the original roll are both
superseded. Everything else about the world (soft edges, the serif and sans
pairing, the panel treatment) is unchanged. Seed key `20f7404a` retained for provenance.

## RAISES

- **Total palette commitment** — donor: WPA park poster. A closed ink set: paper,
  graphite, one rule, steel blue in two steps, slate. Nothing else is admitted.
- **Unapologetic numeric density** — donor: Ikeda datamatics. Tabular figures set
  tight in real columns. Financial data is packed to be compared, not spaced to
  look calm.
- **State as threshold** — donor: darkroom safelight bay. Draft to shared is a
  crossing with visible consequence, not a status string.
